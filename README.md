# Subtitles
These subtitles are for J.A. Vazquez's Malfoid AU, which was created using AI. A big thanks to him for creating these awesome movie length videos.

# What is Malfoid AU
Malfoid is a global online fanfiction trend from late 2026 which rewrites J.K. Rowling's Draco Malfoy from the Harry Potter books with a female Malfoy, often renamed Draconia (as in this AU), Dracaena, or kept as Draco. Whatever makes Ron laugh.

In these alternate retellings, most of Rowling's original story arcs are intact, but Malfoy becomes the love interest for Harry over the course of the multi-year story. Malfoy's backstory is also greatly expanded with her aristocratic upbringing and has more involvement in the core chapters. Every creator (AI or not) have their own vision for how the Malfoid alternate universes unfold.

Malfoid is the term for the trend. The subtitles will still refer to the character as a Malfoy, even if the audio sometimes say Malfoid.

## AI garbage
AI makes mistakes. The subtitles are written to what they would or should be saying, rather than what the audio actually is. This includes some small grammatical errors, even if the sentence sounds right in English. The hope is these subtitles will help enhance the viewing experience of AI content, help with the study of English, or for traditional accessibility reasons that subtitles would be for. Perhaps someday they could be used to correct the audio itself.

There are four lines in the Yule Ball subs that have what I call real AI garbage - Chinese sentences that are mispronounced with English accent and passed off as if it were English. They're funny, and an unforunate side effect of using open & Chinese based models. I've used what the English translation of it should be. However, it is guesswork. Since I'm not the author of the script, it may not be what the author intended.

Fortunately, Vazquez has improved his script for AU Part 1 and AU Part 2. They mostly only contain AI mispronunciations.

## File format
These are Aegisub files. Most media players should be able to use it. All you need to do is put the subtitles in the same folder, and named the same as the video (except the extensions). If it cannot play them, use an online converter to convert it to .SRT format.

## Fonts
These subtitles are styled to look similar to Youtube, with a solid background instead of default 80% opacity. You can download the Roboto font from Google.

## Contribution Notes
I do not consider these "my" subtitles. They're Vazquez's script. So there's no need to credit me. However, the files themselves have my formatting and content decisions, so please adhere to the following.

### British English
This is a proudly British franchise, so let's keep it that way. I've done my best to use British English where possible. Some Americanisms may exist and are hard for me to avoid as I tend to use both randomly, for I am neither British nor American. Feel free to send a pull request if you want it fixed.

Pull requests for Sorcerer's Stone, if it will be used in future creations, will not be accepted.

### Song subtitles
There are no song or karaoke lyrics. The lyrics are copyrighted. Pull requests containing lyrics will not be accepted. You're welcome to make a fork instead.

### Closed captions
There are no closed captions (subtitles describing non-speech audio). Pull requests containing closed captions will not be accepted. You're welcome to make a fork instead.

### Translations
This repo focuses on English only. You're welcome to make a translation or fork, AI or manual, using these as the base. Please ensure you respect the author's (Vazquez's, not me) wishes that you credit him for his works if you're going to hard-sub them.

### Other creators
Most Malfoid AU creators are choosing to create Japanese audio for their animated videos. They are usually already subtitled, since they are targetted toward the non-Japanese speaking audience. I don't feel a need to sub them separately. Though I will continue to be on the lookout (if I haven't abandoned this project already) for any new English creations.
